# Code of Conduct

The [GenAI Red Team Lab](https://github.com/GenAI-Security-Project/GenAI-Red-Team-Lab/) is part of the OWASP GenAI Security Project. We are committed to fostering an open, vendor-neutral, educational, and technically rigorous environment for AI security practitioners, researchers, and developers.

To ensure the integrity, neutrality, and quality of this project, all contributors and participants are expected to adhere to the following standards.

---

## 1. No Self-Promotion or Commercial Promotion

Contributions must be strictly focused on advancing AI red teaming, security research, and education.

*   **No Self-Promotion**: No self-promotion is allowed.
*   **Vendor Neutrality**: No promotion of one's employer, commercial products, services, or companies is allowed.

    Tutorials, sandboxes, and exploitation examples should not serve as marketing materials or product placement.
    
    Comparisons and evaluations that involve commercial products or companies must remain fair and objective.

## 2. Proper Attribution and Referencing

Transparency and intellectual honesty are fundamental to our work.

*   **Cite Sources**: Always reference original work, authors, research papers, datasets, blog posts, and foundational repositories when introducing ideas, attack methodologies, or defense strategies.
*   **Clear Lineage**: Clearly credit any upstream research or prior art upon which your contribution builds.

## 3. Zero Tolerance for Plagiarism

*   **Originality**: Submissions must reflect genuine work by the contributor or properly licensed, attributed integration.
*   **No Misrepresentation**: Do not pass off others' work, analysis, writeups, prompts, or code as your own. Plagiarism in any form will result in the immediate rejection or removal of the contribution.

## 4. No Direct Copying of External Content or Repositories

*   **Do Not Duplicate External Projects**: Avoid copying tools, scripts, or libraries developed elsewhere (such as personal repositories, corporate internal code, or third-party open-source projects) directly into this codebase.
*   **Donations to OWASP**: If you are the author of a standalone tool and wish to contribute or donate it to OWASP, it must proceed through proper OWASP project donation and intellectual property transfer channels rather than being duplicated into this repository.
*   **Integration via Standards**: Where external tools are leveraged, integrate them as documented dependencies or automated scanning workflows (as detailed in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)), provided they meet our criteria for public scrutiny and community vetting.

## 5. Enforcement and Reporting

The project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Contributions that violate this Code of Conduct may be modified or rejected.

If you observe behavior or contributions that violate these guidelines, please contact the repository maintainers or open an issue for review.
